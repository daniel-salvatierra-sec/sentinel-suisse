# Permit G (cross-border)

This **is not immigration advice**. We do not promise visas. Always read SEM.

## In short
Permit **G** is for people who live in a neighbouring country (EU/EFTA) and work in Switzerland. The Confederation sets the rules, not LinkSwiss.

## Official links
- SEM, permit G: [sem.admin.ch]({sem_g})
- EU/EFTA citizens: [ch.ch]({ch_ch})

## On the CV and profile
Say it clearly: G, B, C, L, or none yet. On LinkSwiss: **Account** → permit, and on a job listing → **CV for this job**.
