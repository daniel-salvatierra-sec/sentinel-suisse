# Rental dossier in Switzerland

This **does not get you the home** and does not replace the agency form. Sentinela is not a lawyer.

## What they usually ask for
- A letter for **this** listing, in the listing’s language
- Personal form (Selbstauskunft)
- Debt-enforcement extract (**less than 3 months** old) from the municipality where you live
- 2 or 3 payslips, or the contract
- ID or permit (G, B, C, L…)

## If you live across the border
There is no Swiss extract. Say so clearly and attach the equivalent. Rent is in CHF; pay is often in EUR.

## Official links
- Citizen portal: [ch.ch]({ch_ch})
- In your municipality, look up «Betreibungsamt» / «office des poursuites». Skip expensive middlemen if the office issues it itself.
