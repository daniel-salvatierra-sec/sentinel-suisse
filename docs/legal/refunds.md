# Refund policy — LinkSwiss Premium (provisional)

**Status:** Live — LinkSwiss Premium is billed via Stripe in live mode  
**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Scope

Applies to **LinkSwiss Premium** subscriptions paid via Stripe (card and TWINT when enabled).

## Cooling-off / refunds

1. You may request a **full, unconditional refund** within **14 days** of the first charge — whether or not you used Premium features (WhatsApp channel, extra saved searches, etc.) in that period. This mirrors the French/EU right of withdrawal (*droit de rétractation*, Code de la consommation Art. L221-18) and applies regardless of your country of residence.
2. After the 14-day period, subscriptions renew until cancelled via **Compte → Gérer l’abonnement** (Stripe Customer Portal) or support; charges already collected for the current billing period are non-refundable except at our discretion.
3. Refunds are processed via Stripe to the original payment method (typically 5–10 business days).

## How to request

Email the privacy/contact address listed in the Terms with your account email and Stripe receipt.

## Chargebacks

Disputed payments may suspend Premium until resolved. Fraudulent chargebacks may lead to account closure.

This document is not legal advice; update with counsel before charging customers in Switzerland.
